The maker

Fashion meets robotics.

A self-taught sewing hobby that became an autonomous pattern-drawing robot, and then this shop.

Having taught myself to sew bespoke garments, I wanted to combine that hobby with my interest in robotics. So I designed and built an autonomous robot that draws out trouser patterns from a set of body measurements.

Autonomous Robotics System

Fashion design and robotics · Oct 2024 – Feb 2025

  • Design and softwareI modelled the chassis in SolidWorks and wrote embedded C++ for an Arduino microcontroller, structured as a finite-state machine.
  • Solving the hard problemA hardware fault was costing the robot its drive traction. I re-engineered the voltage step-down with a new motor driver and switched to Mecanum wheels, so every pattern it draws is precise and consistent.
  • From workshop to webI then taught myself HTML, CSS, Python and PostgreSQL to launch this shop, with product listings, payments, customer feedback and secure accounts.

Every piece here is still cut and sewn by hand.
